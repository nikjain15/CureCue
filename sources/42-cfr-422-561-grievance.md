# 42 CFR § 422.561 — Definitions (excerpt)

Retrieved 2026-10-25 from https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/422.561 (Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School). Federal regulations are public domain.

> **Grievance** means any complaint or dispute, other than one that constitutes an organization determination, expressing dissatisfaction with any aspect of an MA organization's or provider's operations, activities, or behavior, regardless of whether remedial action is requested.

Used by CureCue for: the rule that dissatisfaction counts as a grievance even when the member never says the word (`P-GRV` in `rules.js`), and as the source for every test line labelled "member unhappy".
