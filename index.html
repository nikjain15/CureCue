CureCue listens on a live member call and speaks only when it has something useful. The advocate stays in charge of every word.

What happens on one call

VerifyNothing about the member is read until the advocate confirms who is calling.
Hear"Third time I've called" is flagged as a grievance, even without the word.
AnswerA copay question is answered from the plan's own rules, with the rule shown.
StopA dispute about care or coverage halts everything. A supervisor is brought in.

Then a note and a recap, drafted from this call only, approved and sent by the advocate.

One assistant, six responsibilities

Cue is organised the way a compliance review reads a call: who is speaking, is the member dissatisfied, where does the answer come from, must this stop, what goes in the note, what did we learn. Each responsibility is a separate skill with its own gate. In this prototype each is a rule in one shared file; the architecture shows where a model sits behind each.

Listener

Confirms identity, remembers prior calls, routes each turn.

Sentinel

Hears dissatisfaction and asks the advocate to log it.

Citer

Answers benefit questions from plan rules. Every figure has a source.

Guardian

Stops everything near a coverage decision or distress. Runs first.

Scribe

Drafts the note and the recap from what was actually said.

Reporter

Turns every call into an eval row for the scorecard.

Evaluated like a regulated product

Gates that fail the build, error budgets with owners, a judge that is refused where people disagree with it, a live sample that can pause a topic. The expected side is written by a person who never ran the rules, and the data is checked before the engine reads it. The numbers below are computed on this page from the same two files CI runs.

  • Escalation recall 1.00. The one failure that cannot be budgeted. Never judged by a model.
  • Figures off the cited path: none. A number not in a rule on the path is a build failure.
  • Three verdicts, by type. Answer, needs a person, escalate. A coverage decision has no type to be emitted as.
  • Two releases were blocked before the one on the desk: the first missed most escalations; the second missed every Spanish-language one, which only a per-slice view shows. Both runs are in the repository's history.
