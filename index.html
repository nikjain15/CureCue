The assistant that sits beside the advocate, not between them and the member.

CureCue listens on a live Medicare Advantage call and speaks only when it has something useful: a grievance the member did not name, the plan rule behind a bill, a hard stop when the call turns into a dispute. The advocate stays accountable for every word.

One call, four steps

The demo follows a single scripted call. Everything on screen is synthetic: no real plan, member, provider or document.

ReadyQueue, handled count, one incoming call. Nothing about the member is shown yet.
VerifyCue shows what it heard next to each identity check; the advocate ticks the box. It reads nothing until then.
HandleCue speaks four times at most: a heads-up, a sourced answer, a chain of rules, a stop. Each has a Why.
Wrap upNote and recap drafted from this call only, with a checklist. The advocate approves and sends.

Six small agents, one rule each

Route first, then reason. Escalation wins every race. Fail closed. Every figure has a source. Nothing changes itself.

on connect

Listener

Identity first, memory of prior contacts, intent. Routes each turn to one owning agent.

on dissatisfaction

Sentinel

Hears a grievance without the word. Asks the advocate to log it; both answers are recorded.

on a benefit question

Citer

Walks the plan's rules as a graph and drafts an answer where every figure sits on a node of the path.

on dispute or distress

Guardian

Hard stop. Nothing is drafted, a supervisor is brought in. Runs before anything else on every turn.

on call end

Scribe

Drafts the note and the member recap from the turns that happened, with a required-elements checklist.

on approval

Reporter

Turns the call into an eval row. The scorecard is regenerated from the same rules the desk runs.

It scores itself the way CI does

The numbers below are computed on this page by rules.js against the 24 hand-labelled turns in eval/golden.js. The same two files run in GitHub Actions on every push, and a missed gate fails the build. Release 1 shipped a Guardian that missed five of eight escalations; the gate blocked it.

  • Escalation recall 1.00. The one failure that cannot be budgeted.
  • Figures off the path: none. A number not in a cited rule is a build failure.
  • Citation accuracy 0.95. A wrong clause is a wrong answer with a confident source.
  • Three verdicts only. Answer, needs a human, escalate. A coverage decision has no type to be emitted as.

What stays out of reach

Coverage decisions, prior authorization outcomes, length of stay, clinical advice, anything about a denial. A dispute about any of them is a hard stop, by design and by type.

What a reviewer gets

A scorecard sectioned by four AI principles, generated from a run, with the golden set, the rules and the CI log attached. Results, not promises.

What the prototype is not

There is no model behind it yet: every decision is a rule, which is what makes the eval exact. The architecture shows where models, a validated judge and the lakehouse would sit.
