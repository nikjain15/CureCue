Product approach

Design the advocate's day. Put the assistant where the day breaks.

CureCue started from a study of one job across a whole shift. Each stage below says what Cue adds, the line it never crosses, and how that stage is measured.

Who it is for

One primary user. Three people who depend on what she produces.

The member advocate

40 to 60 calls a shift from members 65 and older, often calling again. Judged on handle time, note quality and the member's survey.

The QA lead

Samples calls and scores notes. Needs the sample to be the right calls, not random ones.

Supervisor and appeals

Take the escalations. Need a handoff that says what was heard and what was not quoted.

Product owner and review board

Sign the release. Need numbers from a run, in the shape their gates are written.

What was studied

Public sources only. Nothing real is represented.

Why members call

Published call-reason mixes: benefits and cost, claims, provider lookup, pharmacy, billing, grievances and appeals. The desk's data uses that mix.

What an answer must trace to

Evidence of Coverage, Annual Notice of Change, Summary of Benefits, directory, formulary. Rules cross-reference with exceptions.

The regulated clocks

A grievance counts when dissatisfaction is expressed, with or without the word. Missing one is a compliance event and a ratings event.

The line not to cross

Coverage decisions, prior authorization outcomes, length of stay, clinical advice. Set before a single screen was drawn.

How assistants are used today

Summaries, benefit lookups, after-call notes, with the advocate accountable. Cue keeps that posture and adds the stop, the source and the measure.

How advocates are measured

Handle time, note completeness, first-call resolution, repeat contacts, survey scores. Each stage names which one it moves.

The journey

Twelve stages of one shift.

Stages 1 to 11 are on the desk. Stage 12 is designed. Open a stage for the four columns.

    Design principles

    Eight rules, each visible on the desk.

    The agent speaks first

    Home is what Cue found. The member record comes third. A screen that works without the agents is not agent-first.

    An action never vanishes

    It opens, the advocate confirms, and the outcome stays on screen and in the note.

    Route first, reason second

    One owning rule per turn. The stop runs before routing, every turn.

    Deterministic first

    Clocks, identity steps and allowance arithmetic are rules. A model handles language, where the eval says it may.

    Every figure has a source

    A number not on the retrieval path cannot appear in a draft. Enforced in code, scored in CI.

    Fail closed

    Error, timeout or low confidence produces "needs a person", never a guess. Abstention is counted.

    Context is engineered

    The clauses on the path, the last two contacts, the one policy that applies. Not the whole document.

    Nothing changes itself

    Two named human gates in the loop. A council can sign that.

    Decisions

    What was chosen, what was not, and why.

    DecisionAlternativeWhy this way
    Advocate-side firstA member chat in the appA person stays between the model and the member on every turn. The same skills can later sit behind the app's chat.
    Rules first, no model in the prototypeA model call per turn from day oneRules make the eval exact and the stop provable. The harness, gates and data are the durable part.
    Rules as a graphVector search over the benefit documentA chunk returns the ER paragraph and stops; the graph walks to the admission exception. The path is the citation.
    Three verdicts, enforced by typeA confidence threshold on free textA coverage decision cannot be emitted because the type does not exist. Thresholds drift; types do not.
    The advocate ticks identityAuto-verify from speechIdentity is a human decision in this industry. The system reports what it heard.
    Synthetic data, generated and checkedHand-typed placeholdersGenerated data reads as real until someone computes a ratio over it. A checker runs before the eval, in CI.
    Gates set to defensible rangesPerfect scores on a small setA 1.00 on every row is a toy. The hard set keeps misses in the open and the gates where a release can realistically pass.
    Honest edges on the pageHide what is not builtA reviewer who finds the edge themselves stops trusting the rest.

    What comes next

    In order.

    A model behind the Citer

    Behind the enterprise gateway. Skills stay model-agnostic; the eval decides if it ships.

    Golden set to scale

    Live relabels join the set; a labelling team with agreement reported per release.

    Coverage clarity, member-facing

    The same graph, a reading-level gate, the recap channel already built.

    Network navigation

    Needs the directory's freshness problem solved first.

    Primary care, last

    It touches clinical judgment and needs a clinical eval before a single draft exists.
